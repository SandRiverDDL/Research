## Availability / Openness

- Hugging Face repository: public, non-gated, approximately 2.78T parameters and 1.56 TB stored artifacts at intake time。
- License permits use, modification, redistribution, fine-tuning and derivative works, but it is a custom Kimi K3 License。
- MaaS businesses exceeding USD 20M aggregate revenue over a consecutive 12-month period need a separate Moonshot agreement for commercial use。
- Commercial products exceeding 100M MAU or USD 20M monthly revenue must prominently display “Kimi K3”。
- Therefore this dossier uses `open-weights`, not `fully-open` or OSI-style `open-source`。
