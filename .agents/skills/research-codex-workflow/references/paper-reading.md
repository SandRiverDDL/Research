# Paper Reading

Use this for single-paper reading, lightweight notes, or projects without a batch card pipeline.

## Reading Output

Prefer concise Chinese notes with English technical terms. Keep direct quotations in English when useful.

Template:

```markdown
# Paper

Title:
Venue / Year:
Code / Data:
PDF / HTML:
Zotero:

## One-line Claim

## Core Idea

## What Is Actually New

## Method

## Evaluation
Benchmarks:
Baselines:
Metrics:
Main results:

## Weaknesses

## Relevance to My Work

## Follow-up Ideas
```

## L1 vs L2

L1 is a research index:

- what the paper contributes
- why it matters
- how it relates to the map
- source anchors

L2 is deep reading:

- full mechanism reconstruction
- detailed evidence tables
- reproduction notes
- subjective verdicts
- experiment ideas
- writing-ready related-work material

Do not expand L1 into a full survey.

## Source Discipline

Separate:

- fact: directly supported by source
- interpretation: your explanation of the source
- guess: plausible but not verified
- open question: needs more evidence

When evidence is thin, say so instead of filling gaps confidently.
