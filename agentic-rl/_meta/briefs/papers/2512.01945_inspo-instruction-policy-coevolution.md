In the pioneering context of RL, different from traditional IO approaches that optimize the instruction before commencing the RL train- ing (Zhang et al., 2025), INSPO is the first framework to integrate IO directly into the online RL loop, co-evolving instructions and the policy model with on-policy experience and environmental feedback. 7. Conclusion In this work, we introduced INSPO, a novel framework that enables the co-evolution of instructions and policy learn- ing for agentic policy optimization. Different to existing RLVR approaches that rely on static, manually-designed instructions, INSPO maintains a dynamic population of in- struction candidates and employs an experience-driven in- struction generation mechanism, automating the discovery of more effective reasoning strategies via reflecting on on- line feedback along the policy learning process. Our exper- iments demonstrate that INSPO substantially outperforms strong baselines with static instructions on a wide range of tool-integrated question-answering benchmarks. The case studies and ablation analyses further confirmed that the on- line, reflection-based co-evolution of instructions is key to guiding the agent toward more robust and accurate tool- integrated reasoning. INSPO represents a significant step toward creating a more autonomous and adaptive RL frame- work, reducing the burden of manual prompt engineering and unlocking new capabilities in LLM agents. 8
