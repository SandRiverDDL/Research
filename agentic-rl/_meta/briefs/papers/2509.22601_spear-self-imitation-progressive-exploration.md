# Bounded Brief: SPEAR

Source: `papers/2509.22601-spear-self-imitation-progressive-exploration.pdf`

## Key Evidence

- [p.1] SPEAR is introduced as self-imitation learning for agentic LLMs: replay buffer stores good experience for off-policy update; curriculum steers policy entropy across stages; intrinsic reward shaping accelerates tool-use exploration.
- [p.2-p.3] The paper frames the problem as exploration/exploitation in long-horizon sparse-reward agent tasks, where entropy maximization can cause collapse or divergence under multi-turn distribution shift.
- [p.5-p.6] SPEAR keeps trajectories with positive advantages in a replay buffer and recalibrates off-policy advantages using a FIFO baseline buffer; the total objective is `J_GRPO + gamma * J_SIL`.
- [p.7] Intrinsic reward shaping adds outcome, tool-call, and format rewards, then decays the tool-call reward to avoid reward hacking via unnecessary tool use.
- [p.8-p.9] On ALFWorld and WebShop, SPEAR improves GRPO/GiGPO/Dr.BoT by up to 16.1/5.1/8.6 and 20.7/11.8/13.9 points respectively.
- [p.23-p.24] Algorithm 1 summarizes replay buffer, baseline buffer, intrinsic reward shaping, on-policy GRPO update, off-policy SIL update, covariance clipping, and reset logic; the paper says SPEAR is compatible with GRPO and GiGPO.
