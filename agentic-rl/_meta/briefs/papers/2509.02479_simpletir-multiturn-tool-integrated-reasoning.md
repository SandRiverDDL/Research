# Bounded Brief: SimpleTIR

Source: `papers/2509.02479-simpletir-multiturn-tool-integrated-reasoning.pdf`

## Key Evidence

- [p.1-p.2] SimpleTIR targets unstable multi-turn tool-integrated reasoning under RL; the paper attributes collapse to distributional drift from external tool feedback, which creates low-probability tokens that compound over turns.
- [p.3] The unified policy is trained with GRPO, and feedback token masking excludes environment-provided feedback tokens from the gradient.
- [p.6] The key algorithmic step is void-turn filtering: if a trajectory has any turn with neither a complete code block nor final answer, the policy loss for the whole trajectory is masked before the GRPO update.
- [p.6] The authors argue this blocks high-magnitude gradients from low-probability tokens and prevents successful early turns from receiving negative credit due to later collapse.
- [p.7-p.9] SimpleTIR is evaluated on math/tool-interpreter benchmarks from base models and shows stable training compared with naive multi-turn training and low-probability/high-ratio filters.
- [p.10-p.11] Related work positions SimpleTIR against DAPO, Dr.GRPO, Search-R1, ReTool, ToRL and ZeroTIR; limitations include max 10 turns, reliance on parallel sandbox, and open issues in asynchronous rollout, memory and credit assignment.

## Classification

SimpleTIR is core-with-reasoning/tool-risk: the mechanism is a transferable GRPO trajectory-filtering and feedback-masking rule, but the empirical setting is mostly math tool-integrated reasoning rather than broad web/GUI agents.
