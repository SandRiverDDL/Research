# Causal Micro-Probes Predict Which Teacher–Student–Data Triads Distill

**Method.** Teacher-Causal Response Probe (TCR-Probe)

## Motivation
**Problem framing.** Across on-policy distillation (OPD), teacher usefulness is a property of a teacher–student–data triad, not of teacher strength alone. A student must visit prefixes on which the teacher has outcome-relevant knowledge, receive a target that remains learnable at its current capacity, and convert that local signal into final task gain. The Phase 0 literature isolates pieces of this chain: openreview:TteFp6TLUA questions whether a frozen teacher is learnable at student states, arxiv:2604.13016 studies thinking-pattern overlap and recoverable teacher knowledge, and arxiv:2605.13643 plus arxiv:2605.30833 diagnose position-dependent teachability and supervision-fidelity decay.

The unresolved bottleneck is prospective transport: none of these within-pair diagnostics establishes a score that can be computed before a full OPD run and used without recalibration across unseen teacher families, student capacities, and data distributions. Raw early gain is also insufficient because it mixes teacher-specific transfer with generic adaptation to the probe buffer. TCR-Probe reframes distillability as a matched functional response: compare identical micro-training arms that differ only in whether targets come from the candidate teacher or the frozen student, then divide the excess response by verifier-measured teacher headroom. This makes the load-bearing question explicit—whether the fraction of available teacher advantage that becomes student improvement is stable enough to rank triads.

**Why now.** The gap is timely because several 2026 studies now expose the ingredients needed for a controlled prospective measurement: arxiv:2604.13016 operationalizes student-reachable teacher knowledge, arxiv:2605.13643 identifies local teachability collapse, arxiv:2605.30833 supplies verifier-based handoff and fidelity measurements, and arxiv:2606.09471 warns that low KL agreement can be misleading on degraded prefixes. At the same time, open-weight Qwen3 and DeepSeek-R1-Distill models, public DAPO-Math-17K and MATH data, and exact-answer verifiers make matched micro-training arms reproducible without paid APIs. What is newly possible is not another post hoc correlational proxy, but a controlled TEACH-versus-SELF intervention whose score can be tested unchanged under held-out teacher-family and data shifts.

**Why prior work stopped.**
- `openreview:TteFp6TLUA` (acl-arr-2026-may 2026): openreview:TteFp6TLUA examined whether a frozen stronger teacher remains learnable on student-visited states when teacher and student capacities differ.
  - _Did not do_: It did not construct a matched TEACH-versus-SELF micro-training response or test whether that response ranks full-run gains across unseen triads.
  - _Structural reason_: Its analysis lacked a counterfactual control that separates teacher-specific transfer from generic short-run student adaptation.
- `arxiv:2604.13016` (arXiv 2026-04): arxiv:2604.13016 measured thinking-pattern overlap, teacher new knowledge, and gap recovery in controlled within-family OPD comparisons.
  - _Did not do_: It did not turn those observations into an unrefit prospective score validated under teacher-family, capacity-ratio, and data-distribution holdouts.
  - _Structural reason_: It lacked an intervention-based estimand that normalizes realized student response by the teacher headroom available on the same student-state buffer.
- `arxiv:2605.13643` (arXiv 2026-05): arxiv:2605.13643 used student-reachable teacher margin to identify where supervision should fade along a reasoning trajectory.
  - _Did not do_: It did not measure whole-triad distillability before training or compare candidate teachers through matched micro-training arms.
  - _Structural reason_: Its measurement was designed for position-wise control inside one run rather than transportable triad-level selection.
- `arxiv:2605.30833` (arXiv 2026-05): arxiv:2605.30833 diagnosed supervision-fidelity decay using teacher confidence and prefix-handoff completion and used lookahead reward to steer training.
  - _Did not do_: It did not isolate excess learning caused by a candidate teacher over a frozen-self control or validate that quantity against cross-triad final gain.
  - _Structural reason_: Its signals measure target quality and local corrective opportunity, but not the student's causal functional response to consuming that opportunity.

**What changes when the gap closes.** A validated response score would let researchers reject unpromising teacher–student–data triads before committing to full OPD and would make teacher choice an auditable selection decision rather than a strength heuristic. It would also connect the local-teachability findings of arxiv:2604.13016, arxiv:2605.13643, and arxiv:2605.30833 to a stronger empirical statement: under stated transport conditions, normalized teacher-specific micro-response predicts which triads convert local headroom into final exact-answer gain. Conversely, failure would show that distillability is too path-dependent for a fixed small-budget surrogate, redirecting effort toward adaptive within-run diagnostics.

## Method
**Pipeline.** For each candidate teacher–student–data triad c, freeze the initial student and collect a shared buffer Bc of student-generated prefixes plus a held-out evaluation slice. Measure how much verifier-scored outcome headroom the candidate teacher exposes on those same prefixes. Fork the identical student checkpoint and buffer into matched TEACH and SELF micro-training arms, differing only in whether cached targets come from the candidate teacher or the frozen initial student. Convert the arms' excess outcome response into the normalized score R(c), then use that score without refitting to rank triads under teacher-family and data holdouts. The score survives only if it predicts full-OPD gain better than the named static and naive-early-gain baselines and loses that value when teacher targets are prefix-permuted.

### background
*Background: freeze shared student states and establish the available verifier-measured teacher headroom.*

1. **Freeze the triad and build matched states** (`S1`)
   - Freeze a byte-identical initial student checkpoint $\theta 0$ together with its tokenizer, prompt template, decoding configuration, and random seed. Enumerate the selected public task dataset Dc with stable example identifiers and create a leakage-group identifier for examples that share a source, template, or derivation; assign whole leakage groups to the rollout pool or the fixed evaluation slice Ec, never both. 【author decision: name Dc and lock the split unit and buffer/evaluation proportions so related instances cannot cross the split.】 Run $\theta 0$ on each rollout-pool example and store every generated trajectory as a record containing $triad_{id}, example_{id},$ input x, reference-answer handle, prompt tokens, generated token identifiers, token log probabilities, and the selected prefix endpoint p. 【author decision: lock the rollout decoding distribution and the prefix-endpoint sampling rule that defines the early-student state distribution.】 Materialize Bc as an immutable table of (x,p) records and Ec as an immutable table of complete evaluation examples; write hashes of $\theta 0,$ the tokenizer, both tables, and every sampling seed to a manifest reused by all later arms.
   - _Why:_ Holding states, examples, and initialization fixed prevents teacher comparisons from inheriting different rollout distributions before the causal contrast is formed.
2. **Measure available teacher headroom** (`S2`)
   - For every immutable (x,p) record in Bc, reconstruct the exact prompt-plus-prefix token sequence and submit that identical sequence to the candidate teacher Tc and frozen student $\theta 0.$ Produce one completion from each source under a locked decoding rule, retain the completion token identifiers and stop reason, parse the final answer with the task's deterministic canonicalizer, and apply the exact-answer verifier V to obtain a scalar in {0,1}. Calculate Hc as the equal-weight mean of the paired teacher-minus-student verifier scores, and retain the per-record pair so the aggregate is reproducible. In the same record, cache the supervision object supplied by each source with its attention mask, token positions, vocabulary mapping, and normalization metadata. 【author decision: choose full next-token probabilities, a declared truncated distribution with residual mass, or sampled target sequences, and lock the teacher interface and cache schema required by that choice.】 Reject records only for declared interface failures, log their identifiers and reasons, and derive both the teacher and frozen-self caches from the same retained Bc index set.

*Verifier-measured teacher handoff headroom on the frozen initial-student prefix buffer Bc.*
$$ H_c = \frac{1}{|B_c|}\sum_{(x,p)\in B_c}\left[V\!\left(y^{T_c}_{x,p}\right)-V\!\left(y^{S_0}_{x,p}\right)\right] \tag{1} $$

   - _Why:_ The same raw response should mean different things when a teacher exposes little versus substantial outcome-relevant advantage; Hc supplies that denominator.

### matched_causal_response
*Measure teacher-specific short-training response with matched TEACH and SELF arms, then normalize it by available headroom.*

3. **Run the matched TEACH and SELF probes** (`S3`)
   - Create $\theta cTEACH$ and $\theta cSELF$ as byte-identical copies of $\theta 0$ and attach separate optimizer states initialized from the same configuration. Build one ordered training index over Bc; for every index, TEACH reads Tc's cached supervision object and SELF reads $\theta 0's$ cached object, while input prefixes, example weights, batch boundaries, number of supervised target tokens, update count, learning-rate positions, and random-number streams remain matched. Optimize both arms with the same token-level distillation loss and reduction. 【author decision: lock the loss compatible with the S2 supervision representation, its reduction, optimizer and schedule, and the exact shared stopping point in updates or supervised target tokens.】 The SELF targets always come from the frozen cache and are never refreshed from the evolving SELF checkpoint. Save both final checkpoints plus a manifest containing cache hashes, ordered record identifiers, per-step token counts, optimizer settings, seeds, and training-loss traces, so target source is the only intentional arm-level difference.
   - _Why:_ The SELF arm estimates generic short-run adaptation, leaving target source as the operative difference between arms.
4. **Compute normalized excess response** (`S4`)
   - Evaluate $\theta 0, \theta cTEACH,$ and $\theta cSELF$ on the identical ordered Ec records using the same prompt formatter, decoding rule, answer canonicalizer, verifier V, and seed policy. Store one verifier score per checkpoint and example, and define M as their equal-weight mean over Ec. Calculate $\Delta cTEACH$ and $\Delta cSELF$ from the linked outcome-change definition, then calculate the Teacher-Causal Response R(c) from the linked excess-response definition using the Hc produced in S2. Persist a per-triad record containing Hc, both outcome changes, their raw difference, R(c), the number of valid evaluation records, and hashes of all three checkpoints and Ec. Do not clip or replace Hc. 【author decision: predeclare how Hc $\le  0$ and positive but near-zero Hc are labeled and treated in ranking, including the criterion for an uninterpretable ratio, while always retaining the raw denominator and numerator.】

*Fixed-budget outcome change of each matched micro-training arm on the same evaluation slice.*
$$ \Delta^{a}_c = M\!\left(\theta^{a}_c;E_c\right)-M\!\left(\theta_0;E_c\right),\quad a\in\{\mathrm{TEACH},\mathrm{SELF}\} \tag{2} $$


*Teacher-Causal Response: excess micro-training gain per unit of verified teacher headroom.*
$$ R(c)=\frac{\Delta^{\mathrm{TEACH}}_c-\Delta^{\mathrm{SELF}}_c}{H_c} \tag{3} $$

   - _Why:_ The numerator removes generic early gain while the denominator asks what fraction of available teacher advantage was internalized; surfacing low Hc preserves the estimand's boundary instead of hiding it.

### transport_and_placebo_test
*Test whether the unchanged score ranks held-out triads and whether that value depends on teacher–prefix alignment.*

5. **Rank triads without recalibration** (`S5`)
   - Build one locked triad table with columns $triad_{id}, teacher_{family}, data_{group},$ R(c), Hc, frozen-student exact-answer score, post-OPD exact-answer score, and full-run gain $G_{OPD}(c),$ where each post-OPD model starts from its triad's $\theta 0$ and follows the same fixed 5M-token OPD protocol. Freeze all labels and scores before ranking. 【author decision: publish teacher-family and data-group membership, resolve whether each family statistic is evaluated on the held-out family or on $C_-f$ as written in the linked equation, and lock the pooling or weighting rule across folds.】 Within every locked evaluation candidate set, assign average ranks to ties and calculate the linked Spearman rank agreement between R(c) and $G_{OPD}(c).$ Define top-three selection regret as the $mean G_{OPD}(c)$ of the oracle's three highest-gain triads minus the mean gain of the three triads selected by R(c), with a deterministic $triad_{id}$ tie break. Implement overlap, margin, confidence, teacher-accuracy, and naive-early-gain as one scalar column per triad. 【author decision: define each baseline's exact inputs, formula, aggregation unit, and tie policy before comparing it on the identical candidate sets.】 Report per-fold and aggregate rank agreement and top-three regret for R(c) and every baseline without fitting or recalibrating R(c) on any held-out scores.

*Leave-one-teacher-family-out rank agreement between the unrefit probe score and full-OPD gain.*
$$ \rho_{\mathrm{LOTF}}=\operatorname{Spearman}\!\left(\{R(c)\}_{c\in C_{-f}},\{G_{\mathrm{OPD}}(c)\}_{c\in C_{-f}}\right) \tag{4} $$

   - _Why:_ Cross-holdout ranking, rather than within-family fit, is the required evidence that the response functions as a prospective triad-selection signal.
6. **Break the teacher–prefix alignment** (`S6`)
   - For each triad with |Bc| $\ge $ 2, sort Bc records by a stable hash of $triad_{id}$ and $example_{id},$ then assign every record the candidate-teacher supervision object from the next record in the circular order. This one-position cyclic derangement guarantees no prefix keeps its own teacher target while preserving the target cache as an identical multiset, including token lengths, values, and sampling weights. Leave the input-prefix table, SELF cache, $\theta 0$ initialization, ordered training indices, batch boundaries, update count, optimizer settings, random-number streams, Ec, V, and the original Hc unchanged. Train the placebo TEACH checkpoint using the S3 procedure with only the teacher-cache lookup permuted; evaluate it through S4 and substitute its excess outcome change into the same response definition. Insert the resulting placebo response into the locked S5 triad table and calculate the same fold rankings, Spearman correlations, and top-three regret, retaining the permutation map and hashes so the broken alignment is auditable.
   - _Why:_ If selection value survives after teacher targets no longer correspond to their prefixes, R is tracking a generic learning-curve or target-marginal artifact rather than teacher-causal headroom conversion.
