# Causal Micro-Probes Predict Which Teacher–Student–Data Triads Distill

**Method.** Teacher-Causal Response Probe (TCR-Probe)

## Motivation
In on-policy distillation (OPD), a useful teacher is not simply the strongest model. Whether a teacher helps depends on the particular teacher, student, and data combination. The student needs to visit prefixes where the teacher has knowledge relevant to the outcome, receive a target it can learn at its current capability, and turn that local signal into a final task improvement. Existing work separately studies whether a frozen teacher is learnable at student states, overlap in reasoning patterns and recoverable teacher knowledge, position-dependent teachability, and decay in supervision fidelity.

The missing question is whether a score can be computed before a full OPD run and then used, without recalibration, across unseen teacher families, student capacities, and data distributions. Raw early improvement does not answer this because it combines transfer specific to the teacher with the student's general adaptation to the probe buffer. TCR-Probe compares two otherwise identical short training arms: one uses targets from a candidate teacher and the other uses targets from the frozen student itself. It divides the extra response of the teacher arm by the teacher headroom measured by a verifier. The key question is whether the resulting fraction of available teacher advantage that becomes student improvement is stable enough to rank triads.

This measurement is timely because recent 2026 studies provide the needed pieces: student-reachable teacher knowledge, local teachability collapse, verifier-based handoff and fidelity measurements, and the warning that low Kullback–Leibler (KL) agreement can be misleading on degraded prefixes. Open-weight Qwen3 and DeepSeek-R1-Distill models, public DAPO-Math-17K and MATH data, and exact-answer verifiers also allow reproducible matched micro-training arms without paid application programming interfaces (APIs). The new possibility is a controlled TEACH-versus-SELF intervention whose unchanged score can be tested under held-out teacher-family and data shifts, rather than another after-the-fact correlation.

Earlier work did not make this step. openreview:TteFp6TLUA examined whether a stronger frozen teacher remains learnable on student-visited states when capacities differ, but did not build a matched TEACH-versus-SELF response or test whether it ranks full-run gains across unseen triads; it lacked a control that separates teacher-specific transfer from general short-run adaptation. arxiv:2604.13016 measured reasoning-pattern overlap, teacher new knowledge, and gap recovery within controlled model families, but did not create an unrefit prospective score for teacher-family, capacity-ratio, and data-distribution holdouts; it lacked an intervention-based quantity that normalizes realized student response by teacher headroom on the same student-state buffer. arxiv:2605.13643 used student-reachable teacher margin to decide where supervision should fade along a reasoning trajectory, but did not measure whole-triad distillability before training or compare teachers with matched micro-training arms; its measurement was for position-wise control inside one run rather than triad-level selection that transfers. arxiv:2605.30833 diagnosed supervision-fidelity decay from teacher confidence and prefix-handoff completion and used lookahead reward to steer training, but did not isolate a candidate teacher's extra learning over a frozen-self control or validate it against final gain across triads; its signals describe target quality and local opportunity, not the student's causal response to using that opportunity.

If the response score is validated, researchers could reject unpromising teacher–student–data triads before full OPD and make teacher selection an auditable decision rather than a strength heuristic. It would connect local teachability findings to the stronger claim that, under stated transport conditions, normalized teacher-specific micro-response predicts which triads turn local headroom into final exact-answer improvement. If it fails, that would show distillability is too dependent on the training path for a fixed, small-budget substitute and would redirect work toward adaptive diagnostics during training.

## Method
### background
*Background: freeze shared student states and establish the available verifier-measured teacher headroom.*

1. Freeze the exact initial student checkpoint $\theta 0,$ its tokenizer, prompt format, generation settings, and random seed. Give every example in the chosen public task dataset Dc a stable identifier and a leakage-group identifier for related examples, then place whole groups either in the rollout pool or in the separate evaluation slice Ec. 【author decision: name Dc and lock the split unit and buffer/evaluation proportions so related examples cannot cross the split.】 Generate student trajectories from the rollout pool and save, for each trajectory, the input, reference-answer handle, prompt tokens, generated token identifiers, token probabilities, and the point p where the prefix ends. 【author decision: lock the student generation distribution and the rule for choosing prefix endpoints so Bc has a defined early-student state distribution.】 Store these prefix records as the immutable buffer Bc, store complete evaluation examples as Ec, and record hashes and random seeds so every later arm reads exactly the same objects.
   - _Why:_ Keeping the states, examples, and initialization fixed means teacher comparisons do not start from different rollout distributions before the causal comparison.
2. For each fixed (x,p) record in Bc, rebuild exactly the same prompt and prefix and give it to the candidate teacher Tc and frozen student $\theta 0.$ Generate one completion from each using a locked generation rule, save its tokens and stopping reason, convert its final answer to the task's canonical answer format, and use the exact-answer verifier V to assign 1 for correct or 0 for incorrect. Average the paired teacher score minus student score with equal weight across records to obtain Hc, while keeping every paired score. Also cache each source's training target together with token positions, masks, vocabulary mapping, and normalization information. 【author decision: choose full next-token probabilities, a stated truncated probability distribution with its omitted mass, or sampled target sequences, and lock the teacher interface and cache layout for that choice.】 Use the same successfully processed record identifiers for the teacher and frozen-self caches.

*Verifier-measured teacher handoff headroom on the frozen initial-student prefix buffer Bc.*
$$ H_c = \frac{1}{|B_c|}\sum_{(x,p)\in B_c}\left[V\!\left(y^{T_c}_{x,p}\right)-V\!\left(y^{S_0}_{x,p}\right)\right] \tag{1} $$

   - _Why:_ The same raw response has different meaning when a teacher offers little versus substantial outcome-relevant advantage. Hc provides the denominator for that difference.

### matched_causal_response
*Measure teacher-specific short-training response with matched TEACH and SELF arms, then normalize it by available headroom.*

3. Make two exact copies of $\theta 0:$ the candidate-teacher arm TEACH and the frozen-self arm SELF. Initialize separate optimizer states from the same settings and feed both arms the same ordered Bc records, batches, example weights, number of supervised target tokens, update steps, schedule positions, and random-number streams. TEACH reads the cached targets produced by Tc; SELF reads the targets produced once by frozen $\theta 0,$ never targets from the changing SELF model. Train both with the same token-level distillation rule. 【author decision: lock the training loss that matches the target representation, how token losses are combined, the optimizer and schedule, and the exact shared stopping point.】 Save both trained checkpoints and a manifest of cache hashes, record order, token counts, settings, seeds, and loss traces so the intended difference is only the target source.
   - _Why:_ The SELF arm measures general short-run adaptation, so the source of the targets is the active difference between the two arms.
4. Evaluate $\theta 0, \theta cTEACH,$ and $\theta cSELF$ on the same ordered Ec examples with the same prompt format, generation rule, answer canonicalizer, exact-answer verifier V, and random-seed policy. Save each model's 0-or-1 score on every example and let M be the average score over Ec. Use the linked definitions to calculate each trained arm's change from $\theta 0$ and then the Teacher-Causal Response Probe (TCR-Probe) score R(c), using Hc from step 2 (For each fixed). Save Hc, both changes, their difference, R(c), the number of valid examples, and hashes of the checkpoints and Ec in one triad record. Never clip or replace Hc. 【author decision: define how zero, negative, and positive but near-zero Hc values are labeled and handled in ranking, including when the ratio is considered uninterpretable, while retaining the raw values.】

*Fixed-budget outcome change of each matched micro-training arm on the same evaluation slice.*
$$ \Delta^{a}_c = M\!\left(\theta^{a}_c;E_c\right)-M\!\left(\theta_0;E_c\right),\quad a\in\{\mathrm{TEACH},\mathrm{SELF}\} \tag{2} $$


*Teacher-Causal Response: excess micro-training gain per unit of verified teacher headroom.*
$$ R(c)=\frac{\Delta^{\mathrm{TEACH}}_c-\Delta^{\mathrm{SELF}}_c}{H_c} \tag{3} $$

   - _Why:_ The numerator removes general early gain. The denominator asks what fraction of available teacher advantage became student improvement. Reporting low Hc keeps the boundary of the quantity visible.

### transport_and_placebo_test
*Test whether the unchanged score ranks held-out triads and whether that value depends on teacher–prefix alignment.*

5. Create one fixed table with each triad's identifier, teacher-family label, data-group label, R(c), Hc, frozen-student exact-answer score, exact-answer score after full on-policy distillation (OPD), and their full-run gain $G_{OPD}(c).$ Each full OPD run must start from that triad's $\theta 0$ and use the same fixed 5M-token protocol. Freeze the table before ranking. 【author decision: publish the teacher-family and data-group assignments, resolve whether each family fold is scored on the held-out family or on $C_-f$ from the linked equation, and lock how fold results are combined.】 In each fixed candidate set, give tied values their average rank and calculate Spearman rank correlation between R(c) and $G_{OPD}(c).$ Calculate top-three regret as the oracle top three triads' mean full-run gain minus the mean gain of the three selected by R(c), breaking remaining ties by triad identifier. 【author decision: give overlap, margin, confidence, teacher accuracy, and uncorrected early gain exact per-triad formulas, inputs, aggregation units, and tie rules.】 Apply R(c) and every baseline unchanged to the same folds and report both rank correlation and top-three regret for each.

*Leave-one-teacher-family-out rank agreement between the unrefit probe score and full-OPD gain.*
$$ \rho_{\mathrm{LOTF}}=\operatorname{Spearman}\!\left(\{R(c)\}_{c\in C_{-f}},\{G_{\mathrm{OPD}}(c)\}_{c\in C_{-f}}\right) \tag{4} $$

   - _Why:_ Ranking across holdouts, rather than fitting within one family, is the needed evidence that the response can select triads before training.
6. For every triad with at least two Bc records, sort the records by a stable hash of the triad and example identifiers. Give each prefix the candidate-teacher target from the next record in that circular order. This guarantees that no prefix keeps its own target while preserving exactly the same collection of targets, lengths, values, and sampling weights. Keep the prefixes, SELF targets, $\theta 0$ initialization, training-record order, batches, update steps, optimizer settings, random-number streams, Ec, verifier V, and original Hc unchanged. Train the placebo TEACH checkpoint exactly as in step 3 (Make two exact copies of $\theta 0)$ except for the permuted target lookup, evaluate it as in step 4 (Evaluate $\theta 0),$ and use its excess outcome change in the same response definition. Put the placebo response into the same step 5 (Create one fixed table with $each\dots )$ triad table and calculate the same fold rankings, Spearman correlations, and top-three regret. Save the permutation map and hashes.
   - _Why:_ If selection value remains when teacher targets no longer match their prefixes, R is following a general learning-curve or target-marginal artifact instead of teacher-caused conversion of headroom.
