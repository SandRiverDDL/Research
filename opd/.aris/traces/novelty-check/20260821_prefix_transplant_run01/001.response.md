# Independent novelty review

## Verdict

- Generic Prefix Transplantation: novelty 2/10; reject as a novelty claim.
- OPD-specific cross-checkpoint assay: novelty 5/10; pivot. Current top-venue potential 4/10, rising only if a corrected protocol reveals a strong replicated law.

## Closest prior work

1. *Reasoning that Travels* already uses provider/receiver models, increasing reasoning-prefix curves, free-generation versus force-answer, and analyses of answer leakage, scaffolding, and receiver competence.
2. *Counterfactual Recoverability in On-Policy Distillation* directly asks whether an erroneous OPD state remains correctable through continuation and contrasts continuation with rollback.
3. *Towards Understanding OPD through the Lens of Test-Time Scaling* supplies the retained/learned/forgotten stratification and the small-K versus large-K phenomenon.
4. Existing prefix-OPD papers cover short prefix supervision, teacher/student handoff, prefix replay, and prefix-conditioned continuation value.

No searched paper contains the exact combination of same-ancestry base/OPD checkpoints, directional text-prefix exchange, and capability-shift strata. The components themselves have close direct precedents.

## Structural issue

With strict large-K strata, a forgotten problem has a successful base trajectory but no successful OPD trajectory, while a learned problem has the reverse. Therefore a complete 2x2 successful-prefix exchange matrix exists only for retained problems. Either:

- use directional comparisons with missing cells on learned/forgotten sets; or
- define continuous accessibility-shift strata where both checkpoints have nonzero success probability.

## Main confound

A success-selected prefix is privileged context. High cross-completion shows that a receiver can use externally supplied successful text; it does not prove preservation of the same internal continuation mechanism. Low cross-completion may instead reflect prefix style incompatibility or text-prefix OOD effects. The assay supports claims about behavioral recoverability under text intervention, not direct causal claims about internal route state.

## Minimum defensible delta

A same-lineage OPD assay that separates prefix-provider checkpoint from completion checkpoint and measures directional rescue across K-relative capability-shift strata and prefix progress. The matrix alone is insufficient; a paper requires a robust new empirical law replicated across models, benchmarks, and OPD recipes.

## Recommendation

Pivot from “route initiation versus true competence loss” to “directional accessibility asymmetry under successful text-prefix interventions.” A strong result would be stable early-prefix base-to-OPD rescue on K-forgotten problems that survives incorrect, irrelevant, leakage, and decoding controls.
